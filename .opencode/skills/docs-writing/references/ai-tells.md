# AI-tell catalog

Adapted and condensed from `petergyang/no-ai-slop` (MIT). Keep the writer's point and voice; remove only what makes the prose generic.

## Editing stance

- Read the full draft before touching it.
- Notice the voice: vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, digressions.
- Make the minimum effective edit. Leave strong human sentences alone.
- Do not invent claims, examples, statistics, or opinions.
- Do not make every paragraph equally tidy.
- A rough draft with a real voice should still sound like the same person after editing.

## Words and phrases to cut

Banned outright in most prose: delve, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, robust, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, tapestry, realm, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, intricate, paramount, transformative, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness, ever-evolving.

Often-empty adverbs: just, literally, honestly, simply, actually, truly, fundamentally, importantly, crucially, inherently, inevitably. Cut when they add nothing; keep when they carry real emphasis, uncertainty, or contrast.

Often-empty phrases: it's worth noting, it's important to note, at the end of the day, when it comes to, at its core, in today's world, in the age of, the reality is, the truth is, in terms of, with regard to, in order to, going forward, in this article, let's dive in.

Chinese equivalents to cut when empty: 赋能、抓手、闭环、生态、深度融合、显著提升、全面提升、值得注意的是、需要指出的是、综上所述、总而言之、在当今……背景下、让我们一起……、具有重要意义、发挥重要作用.

## Patterns

**Binary contrasts.** "This is not X. It's Y." / "The question isn't X, it's Y." State Y directly.

**Throat-clearing openers.** "Here's the thing," "Let me be clear," "The uncomfortable truth is." Cut the setup and state the point.

**Faux-insight setups.** "What most people get wrong," "Here's what nobody tells you," "The part everyone misses." Make the claim stand on its own.

**Colon reveals.** A noun phrase, a colon, then a lowercase dramatic reveal. Rewrite as a plain sentence. Use colons for lists, labels, and quotes, not fake drama.

**Superficial analysis.** Trailing `-ing` clauses that pretend to explain meaning: "highlighting," "underscoring," "reflecting," "showcasing." Replace with a concrete consequence.

**Importance puffery.** "Stands as a testament," "marks a pivotal moment," "plays a vital role," "solidifies its position." State the fact; let the reader judge.

**Interpretive metadiscourse.** "That last part matters," "The key point is," "As you can see," "This distinction matters," redundant "In other words." Delete or replace with evidence.

**Weasel attribution.** "Experts agree," "industry reports suggest," "many argue," "studies show." Name the source or cut the claim.

**Fake-strong verbs.** Prefer "is" and "has" when they are clearer. "Serves as a centralized hub" often means "tracks X, Y, and Z in one place."

**Synonym cycling.** If the clear word is right, repeat it. Do not rotate terms for style.

**Negative listing.** "Not X. Not Y. Z." Just say Z.

**Dramatic fragmentation.** "X. And Y. And Z." Use complete sentences.

**Robotic rhythm.** Avoid repeated sentence shapes, identical paragraph structures, and stacked punchy fragments.

**Rhetorical setups.** "What if I told you...", "Think about it:", "Plot twist:", self-answered question/answer pairs.

**Fake-profound kickers.** Cut the final cute metaphor or mic-drop line. End on the clearest concrete sentence already in the draft.

**Summary-recap endings.** "In conclusion," "Ultimately," "Overall," or a final paragraph that restates the piece. End on the last concrete point, takeaway, or next action.

**Formatting slop.** Emoji headings, decorative bold, bullets where prose is better, headers over tiny sections.

**Em dashes.** Do not use them as a default rhythm crutch. In short copy, use none. In longer drafts, one or two are fine if they clearly beat commas, periods, or parentheses.

## Chinese-specific tells

- Mechanical three-part structure: 首先/其次/最后 used as a template rather than real sequence.
- Stacked "不仅……而且……" and "既……又……" without a concrete contrast.
- Abstract noun chains: "能力的提升、体系的完善、生态的构建".
- Empty scene-setting: "在数字化转型的大背景下".
- Fake inclusivity: "让我们一起", "我们可以看到".
- Over-signposting: "接下来，我们将从三个方面展开".
- Restating the heading in the first sentence of every section.
- Closing with "总的来说" plus a paragraph that repeats the body.

Keep real sequence words when the steps are genuinely ordered. Keep hedging when the writer is genuinely uncertain.

## Detect mode

For each finding:

1. Name the pattern.
2. Quote the line.
3. Give a fix in a few words.
4. Do not rewrite the whole draft, score it, or claim it was written by AI.

Single flagged words are not proof. Report clusters and structural patterns, not word bans.

## Self-check

- Does the draft lead with a point rather than a setup?
- Is every abstract claim backed by a fact, example, or mechanism?
- Are repeated sentence shapes and paragraph templates gone?
- Did the edit preserve the writer's voice, including useful edge and uncertainty?
- Are code blocks, quotes, frontmatter, and link targets untouched?
- Does the ending land on a concrete point instead of a recap?
